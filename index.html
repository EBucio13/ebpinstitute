About Us / Acerca de Nosotros

El Buen Pastor Bible Institute is a ministry of El Buen Pastor Church Assemblies of God in Baytown, Texas. We are a study group using the Global University Berean School of the Bible curriculum. Our goal is to help students grow in biblical knowledge and prepare for Christian ministry and service.

El Instituto Bíblico El Buen Pastor es un ministerio de la Iglesia El Buen Pastor Asambleas de Dios en Baytown, Texas. Somos un grupo de estudio que utiliza el currículo Berean School of the Bible de Global University.
